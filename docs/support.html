Getting started

  1. Choose a segment (swipe the list) and, if you like, a workout and rivals: your best rides or a pacer.
  2. Tap START RIDE and wait for the climb to load.
  3. Start pedalling, or tap START. The slope sets your trainer's resistance.
  4. At the finish you see a replay and your result.

Questions

My trainer or heart-rate strap is not found

Allow Bluetooth for Crown Climb (Settings → Crown Climb), wake the trainer by pedalling and close other apps that are connected to it (Zwift, Garmin Connect, the trainer's own app). Watches must have “broadcast heart rate” switched on.

Can I ride without a smart trainer?

Yes. Tap START and set your power with − and + (10 W per tap).

Which trainers work?

Smart trainers with the Bluetooth fitness machine standard (FTMS), for example most current Wahoo, Tacx, Elite, JetBlack and Saris trainers.

How do I switch the fans off?

In the segment menu: “Fans: on / off”, next to “Weather”.
