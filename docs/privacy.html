Privacy policy

HomeSupport

Last updated: 20 November 2026. Applies to the Crown Climb apps for iPhone and Android.

In short

Crown Climb does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter and every ride you finish stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Your rider profile: the name you choose, weight, FTP and appearance of your rider.
  • Your rides: times, power, heart rate and cadence, used for your top list, your ghost rides and the jerseys.
  • Your settings, for example music, fans, weather and the devices you connected.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your own devices: a smart trainer, a heart-rate strap or watch and a Wahoo Headwind fan. It reads power, cadence, speed and heart rate from them and sets the trainer's resistance and the fan's speed. Nothing of this leaves your device.

Real weather

If “Weather” is switched on (it can be switched off in the menu), the app asks the free weather service Open-Meteo for the current weather at the climb you are about to ride. The request contains only the position of that climb, rounded to about 1 km — not your own location. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address; see their privacy policy. The app does not use your location.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
