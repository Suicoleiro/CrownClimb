Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Applies to the Crown Climb apps for iPhone and Android.

In short

Crown Climb has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter and every ride you finish stays on your device. Only when you join an event (riding together with friends) are your rider name and live ride figures shared with the other riders of that event, and only for as long as the event lasts.

What is stored on your device

  • Your rider profile: the name you choose, weight, FTP and appearance of your rider.
  • Your rides: times, power, heart rate and cadence, used for your top list, your ghost rides and the jerseys.
  • Your settings, for example music, fans, weather and the devices you connected.

This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. The one exception is the event described below.

Events: riding together

If you create or join an event with an event code, the app connects to our event server (run on Cloudflare Workers) while you are in the event. It sends:

  • the rider name you chose in the app (it can be any name or nickname),
  • a random number that identifies your app installation, so that you can reconnect after a network break (it is not linked to your phone, your Apple or Google account or any other data),
  • during the ride, four times a second: your distance on the climb, speed, power, cadence and heart rate, and your finish time.

When you create an event, you choose whether it is listed or private. A listed event appears in the event list of all Crown Climb users until it starts, with its climb, start time, the host's rider name and the number of riders waiting. A private event can only be found with its code.

The server passes these figures on to the other riders of the same event only; it does not store your ride figures. The event itself (code, climb, rider names and finish times) is deleted automatically 24 hours after it was created. We do not use these data for anything else and do not pass them on. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address to deliver the data; see the Cloudflare privacy policy. If you never use events, nothing is sent.

Downloading climbs

To keep the app small, most climbs are downloaded the first time you choose them. The files come from GitHub (the service that hosts the app's files); the download contains nothing about you, but GitHub receives your device's IP address, as with any download. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your own devices: a smart trainer, a heart-rate strap or watch and a Wahoo Headwind fan. It reads power, cadence, speed and heart rate from them and sets the trainer's resistance and the fan's speed. Nothing of this leaves your device.

Real weather

If “Weather” is switched on (it can be switched off in the menu), the app asks the free weather service Open-Meteo for the current weather at the climb you are about to ride. The request contains only the position of that climb, rounded to about 1 km — not your own location. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address; see their privacy policy. The app does not use your location.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children; the event figures above are only passed on between the riders of an event and deleted afterwards.
