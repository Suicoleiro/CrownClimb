Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Applies to the Crown Climb apps for iPhone and Android.

In short

Crown Climb has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter and every ride you finish stays on your device, with a few exceptions you choose yourself: when you join an event, your rider name and live ride figures go to the other riders of that event; when you agree to the world list, your best times are shown there; when you connect Strava, your rides can be uploaded to your Strava account; when you ask for a friends' link, your friends can send you a few words and a short recording; when you sign a summit book, your line is shown to other riders.

What is stored on your device

  • Your rider profile: the name you choose, weight, FTP and appearance of your rider.
  • Your rides: times, power, heart rate and cadence, used for your top list, your ghost rides and the jerseys.
  • Your settings, for example music, fans, weather and the devices you connected.

This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. The one exception is the event described below.

Events: riding together

If you create or join an event with an event code, the app connects to our event server (run on Cloudflare Workers) while you are in the event. It sends:

  • the rider name you chose in the app (it can be any name or nickname),
  • a random number that identifies your app installation, so that you can reconnect after a network break (it is not linked to your phone, your Apple or Google account or any other data),
  • during the ride, four times a second: your distance on the climb, speed, power, cadence and heart rate, and your finish time.

When you create an event, you choose whether it is listed or private. A listed event appears in the event list of all Crown Climb users until it starts, with its climb, start time, the host's rider name and the number of riders waiting. A private event can only be found with its code.

The server passes these figures on to the other riders of the same event only; it does not store your ride figures. The event itself (code, climb, rider names and finish times) is deleted automatically 24 hours after it was created. We do not use these data for anything else and do not pass them on. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address to deliver the data; see the Cloudflare privacy policy. If you never use events, nothing is sent.

World list (online leaderboard)

After your first finished ride the app asks whether your times may go on the world list. Only if you say yes, the app sends your best time on a climb to our server (run on Cloudflare Workers) together with your rider name, your average W/kg, the date and the course of the ride (time and distance, so that others can ride against it as a ghost). Everyone using Crown Climb can see the list. Your app is identified only by a random key that never leaves your device in readable form; the list contains no account, e-mail or device information. You can switch it off at any time on the TOP LIST page and remove your time from a climb there.

Crowns

Only if your times go on the world list: with each ride that goes on the list the app also sends your threshold power (FTP) and, if you entered your year of birth, your age group (under 30, 30 to 39, 40 to 49, 50 to 59, 60 and over). The server uses the threshold power and your weight once, to put the ride into one of four classes (A to D by watts per kilo); it keeps the class and the age group with your time, not the threshold power, your weight or your year of birth. The year of birth never leaves the app. The list of crowns shows, for each crown, the rider name, the time and the lead over the next rider. Removing your time from the world list removes your crowns.

Friends on your road

Nothing of this is used until you press “Get my link”. Then the server keeps a mailbox for your app. The page behind the link shows your rider name. What your friends send there is kept on the server and fetched by your app: the name they typed, their words (at most 24 letters) and, if they recorded one, a voice recording of at most 6 seconds. Only your app can read the mailbox. A message is deleted when you remove it in the app, everything is deleted when you close the link, and nothing is kept longer than 180 days. The app also keeps the messages on your device, so that they show without a connection.

For the friend who opens the link: the page needs no account and sets no cookies. The microphone is only used while you record, after your browser asked you. Your words and your recording go to the rider who sent you the link and to nobody else.

Summit book

If you sign the summit book of a climb, your rider name, your line (at most 80 characters), the time of your ride and the date are shown to every rider who opens the book of that climb. You can remove your line in the app at any time. Lines of others can be reported; a reported line disappears on your device at once and for everyone when a second rider reports it. You can also block a rider: all lines of that rider then stay hidden on your device (the list of blocked riders is kept on your device only; each line carries a short anonymous tag of its writer for this). We look through the reported lines, remove those that are not ok, and can keep a rider who wrote them out of the summit books. Lines with links or abusive words are refused. The book keeps the 500 newest lines of a climb.

The view from the summit and the storyteller

Both work on your device with files that come with the app. Nothing is sent. Sources: heights from Terrain Tiles (Mapzen, hosted by Amazon Web Services open data), names of peaks © OpenStreetMap contributors, stories told after articles of the German Wikipedia.

Strava

If you connect Strava, you log in on Strava's own website; Crown Climb never sees your Strava password. To finish the login, Strava sends a one-time code to our server, which exchanges it for your Strava access keys and hands them to your app (kept on the server for at most 10 minutes, until your app collects them). The keys are stored only on your device. When they need renewing, the app asks our server to do it; the server does not keep them. Rides are uploaded from your device directly to Strava, and only when you tap upload. See the Strava privacy policy.

Apple Health and Health Connect

On iPhone you can let Crown Climb save your finished rides to Apple Health, on Android to Health Connect. This is off until you switch it on on the Rider page and allow it in the system's own permission screen. Each finished ride (with a smart trainer) is then saved as an indoor cycling workout with its time, distance, power, heart rate, cadence, speed and an estimate of the active calories. Crown Climb only writes this data and never reads anything from Apple Health or Health Connect. The data goes from the app straight into Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone; it is not sent to our server or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising. You can switch it off in the app at any time, and you can remove Crown Climb's access and its saved workouts in the Health app or in Health Connect.

Downloading climbs

To keep the app small, most climbs are downloaded the first time you choose them. The files come from GitHub (the service that hosts the app's files); the download contains nothing about you, but GitHub receives your device's IP address, as with any download. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your own devices: a smart trainer, a heart-rate strap or watch and a Wahoo Headwind fan. It reads power, cadence, speed and heart rate from them and sets the trainer's resistance and the fan's speed. Nothing of this leaves your device.

Real weather

If “Weather” is switched on (it can be switched off in the menu), the app asks the free weather service Open-Meteo for the current weather at the climb you are about to ride. The request contains only the position of that climb, rounded to about 1 km — not your own location. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address; see their privacy policy. The app does not use your location.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It has no account and no advertising for anyone, including children; the event figures above are only passed on between the riders of an event and deleted afterwards, and the world list, the friends' link and the summit book are only used by those who choose them.
