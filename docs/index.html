Crown Climb lets you ride real Swiss climbs on your indoor bike: the Eigen Climb, Glaubenberg, Root – Michel and more, built from Swiss map data and aerial photos. The slope sets your smart trainer's resistance, fans cheer you on where it gets steep, and you race your own best rides or a pacer.

What you need

  • An iPhone (or Android phone or Windows PC).
  • Optional: a smart trainer with Bluetooth (FTMS), a heart-rate strap or watch, a Wahoo Headwind fan. Without a trainer you set your power by hand.
